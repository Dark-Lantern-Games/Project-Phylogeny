# Setting & Situation R1

- Setting: Luna Novum — Derelict Human Colony on the surface of Enceladus
	- Dwarf planet within the Eltanin System named after the hydrothermal moon of Saturn
- Situation: Salvage team crash landed; PC awakens and doesn't know where rest of team is
	- Five-person team, all four non-PC characters are missing
		- The Mechanic — breaks down or repurposes mechanical salvage
			- Trying to improve family's life, having left family on Luna Novum
			- First character the player can play as
		- The Captain — access to logs and ship systems, discovered and named Enceladus
			- wife was Luna Novum executor
		- The Man at Arms — crafts and upgrades weapons and armor
			- Lost leg to misfired grenade launcher during "the last war"
		- The Navigator — mapping and plotting paths through unknown terrain
			- Ran from gang that murdered their fiancée & family
		- The Doctor — repairs/cures biological injuries, uses human cell therapies
			- Has cancer, wants to find a cure through exotic means
