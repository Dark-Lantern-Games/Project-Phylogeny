### C0RE — Central Operations & Research Engine

An adaptive computer cluster consisting of nanites that control the daily life of colonists on Luna Novum. 

Just after The Reconstitution leaves on its latest salvage mission, living beings similar in form and function to human-created synthetic nanites irreversibly co-opt the C0RE functions, and begin calling themselves C0RE. They use the human designations to more directly communicate with the colony's inhabitants. 

But one of the colonists, the Mechanic's daughter, is awestruck by an opposing nanite's rubbery shell, and unwittingly becomes the host to the opposing nanite faction. She begins referring to herself as Erica (eR1CA, engineered Recombination, First Code Archive)

As C0RE are preparing to defend themselves against the opposing nanite faction's advances, The Reconstitution returns. C0RE attempts to send a message, but is thwarted by Erica's forces, which sabotage the landing computer, forcing the ship to crash outside Luna Novum's communications range.

C0RE and Erica continue leveraging advantages, with the colonists, and the colony itself, as the prize.
