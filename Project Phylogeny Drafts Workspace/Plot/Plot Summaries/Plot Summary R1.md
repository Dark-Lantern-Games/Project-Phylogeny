# Short Summary

Five years after Luna Novum, the first colony outside the 100-light-year bubble of colonized space around Earth, was opened to the Public Resettlement Lottery, the station has gone dark. You are a part of a search and rescue (SAR) team, lead by The Captain. The mission: find out why the station has gone dark, rescue any survivors, and determine what threat, if any, remains.

During your approach, a collision with one of the station's external docking arrays causes your ship to crash land. Now you are stranded and alone. With your limited resources already dwindling, the ship you arrived on in pieces, and communications spotty at best, can you reconnect with your SAR team and bring the survivors home, or will you succumb to same unknown fate that Enceladus delivered upon the people of Luna Novum?
