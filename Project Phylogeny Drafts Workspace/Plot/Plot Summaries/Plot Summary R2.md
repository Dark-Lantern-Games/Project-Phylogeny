###### The Mechanic

Luna Novum, the remote research colony, has gone dark. For you and some of your crewmates,  those colonists are family.

The surface of Enceladus, in the Eltanin System, promised new research and, with it, new discoveries. The Enceladus Resettlement Project promised greater pay for discoveries and salvage. The ERP Lottery gave thousands of families the option to build their new home on the frontier; you and your daughter were the last selected.

Over the past five years, receiving your daughter's daily video messages whenever you were on a salvage mission always helped to brighten your mood and quicken your day. But her last message was four days ago. She was excitedly showing you a super bouncy ball of slime she found outside that morning. Now, all that greets your inquiries is the dull ping of Luna Novum's distress beacon, a constant reminder that *The Reconstitution*'s salvage run has become a search-and-rescue mission.

And you're still two days out. 

You arrive at Luna Novum, but a malfunction with the colony's automated landing procedure shuts down all ship systems, causing a crash-landing. Your world goes dark.

Miraculously, you awake, but the ship around you is in pieces. You are alone, separated from your team with dwindling resources and unreliable communications. You know your daughter is somewhere within Luna Novum. You can *feel* her in there, somewhere; hear her infectious laugh. And you know your crewmates can help you find her, but you don't know where they are, or how much time she has left.
