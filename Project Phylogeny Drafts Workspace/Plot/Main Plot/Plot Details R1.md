# The plot without compromise

You arrive and recover the C0RE, the colony's Central Operational & Research Engine.
You find the C0RE to be filled with alien nanites, which adapted C0RE's to become a nanite implant. C0RE then allows its implanted host to construct and remotely pilot clones of itself. C0RE also enables:
- Adaptation of repurposed collected matter as weapons, armor, and biological enhancements
- Collect Enceladus data to refine the nanite's abilities outside of C0RE
- Assimilate properties of collected material not repurposed - a clawed arm, armored leg, etc.
- The C0RE allows remote piloting of a clone through shared motor and sensory functions similarly to VR. The C0RE pilot remains at the colony's central computer room.

You learn the colonists are being kept safe by the nanites within C0RE, but there is a small group being actively repurposed by the threat the C0RE nanites defeated millennia before.
During your excursions, you find your crewmates, all separated, and learn of the nanite race that lives on Enceladus.
After finding the Doctor, you learn that the opposing nanites have taken control of your daughter's body, and that she is the one leading the charge against the C0RE nanites. 
You learn that once implanted the nanites will eventually overwhelm the host unless they relinquish the symbiotic connection, and that the daughter you knew is already gone. 
After defeating the nanite-infused entity that was once your daughter, your implanted C0RE suggests you insert it into her body so the C0RE nanites can defeat those still within her body. 

If you have advanced your C0RE sufficiently—provided enough biomass, non-living material, exploration data, and opposing nanite clone data—the C0RE nanites win and your daughter has one final moment of lucidity before passing. If not, the opposing nanites overwhelm the C0RE nanites, destroying the symbiotic allies and taking their place.

As you prepare to bury your daughter, you are given the choice to reimplant or not. 

Reimplanting the symbiotic allies relinquishes yourself to their control, but ensures their survival and a new dawn for human advancement.

Reimplanting an enemy core leads to the end of the colonists and symbiotic nanites, while the enemy nanites advance toward human colonized space.
