##### Background details about C0RE nanites

The two factions were locked in a stalemate until humans land on Enceladus
Human biomass (waste, food, metals and other inanimate material) allowed the nanite factions to gain advantages over each other
Five years after the humans' arrival, the Mechanic's daughter finds a glob of nanite-laced rubber. Thinking it to be a toy, she played with it and sent a video to her dad.
Later that evening, the nanites entered her bloodstream and began the events of the game - nearly overtaking the entire colony before the benevolent nanites overtook C0RE.
The enemy nanite faction controlled the rest of the colony's systems, including the landing controls, which forced The Reconstitution down and start the events of the game proper.
